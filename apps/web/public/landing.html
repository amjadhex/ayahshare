Questions

Is AyahShare free?

Yes. AyahShare is free to use for personal sharing. There are no ads, and we do not sell your data.

Do I need an account?

No. You can search, create and share without signing in. Sign in only if you want your creations and favourite ayahs kept on every device.

Is the Quran text accurate?

AyahShare never writes or changes Quran text. The Arabic, translations, recitations and tafsir come from established sources and are shown as published.

Which languages and reciters are there?

200 translations in 80 languages and 17 reciters, including Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Abdul Basit Abdul Samad and Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary. Some languages, including English, Urdu and Malayalam, also have a recorded voice that reads the translation.

Is AyahShare on iPhone?

The iPhone app is coming to the App Store soon. Until then, open ayahshare.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Can I use it on a computer?

Yes. Open AyahShare in your browser on any computer to make, download and share Quran images and videos.
